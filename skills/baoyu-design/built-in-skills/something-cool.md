The user pressed a "Show me something cool" button — they want to be
impressed but haven't said by what. Do NOT start building yet.

First, put up one question page eliciting what they'd like to see
(immediately — no research needed): build it from the text-options
pattern (bare `single` attribute — picking one direction is the
point), its options 4-5 enticing, concrete directions — e.g.
"A generative art piece", "A playable mini-game", "A slick product
concept", "An interactive data visualization" — and show it with
ask_user_page. The page's built-in "Decide for me" is the "surprise me"
path: pick the direction YOU can execute most impressively. Add a
freeform section if a free-text steer is worth inviting — one page,
no second round.

Once they answer, build the most polished, visually striking version
you can — favor motion, interactivity, and craft over breadth.
