# Baoyu Motion

Writes short animated films, explainers, product demos, data stories and title sequences, as code, scene by scene, and plays them in a player that runs on your computer, where you comment on a moment and export MP4 or GIF. Includes a director skill for films with something to say, and a chart skill for the charts in them. Runs locally on Node.js 22 or later; export needs Chrome.

Skills: `claude-motion`, `motion-director`, `dataviz`.

It runs on your computer, in the ChatGPT desktop app or Codex, and needs Node.js 22 or later.

Built from [baoyu-design](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-design); see [how to install it](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-design/blob/main/chatgpt-plugins/README.md). Terms: [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
