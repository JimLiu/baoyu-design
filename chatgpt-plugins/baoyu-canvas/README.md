# Baoyu Canvas

Writes design canvases, artboards side by side, as files in your workspace and opens them in a canvas editor that runs on your computer. Compare options, retype copy, adjust tweaks and comment for the agent. Runs locally on Node.js 20 or later; nothing leaves your computer unless you share it.

Skills: `claude-design`.

It runs on your computer, in the ChatGPT desktop app or Codex, and needs Node.js 20 or later.

Built from [baoyu-design](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-design); see [how to install it](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-design/blob/main/chatgpt-plugins/README.md). Terms: [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
